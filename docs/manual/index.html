User manual

Verbil User Manual

Verbil checks an invoice before a lender pays out against it: is it real, was it delivered, will the buyer pay, and has anyone financed it already. This manual walks through every screen. Yellow numbers in the text match the yellow markers on the screenshots.

WorkspaceWho it's forWhat you do there
Shakti Bank, Kaveri CapitalLendersReview verified applications, see the evidence, pay out, monitor the portfolio and audit trail
Acme Precision PartsA small business (supplier)Upload an unpaid invoice, get an offer, track it to payout
Zenith Motors, Orbit RetailBuyers who owe the moneyConfirm or dispute invoices raised against them
Independent verifierAuditors, anyoneCheck a Verbil record or document against the blockchain
Before you start

Opening the app

  1. 1On the laptop where Verbil is installed, double-click start.bat in the project folder. Two terminal windows open: the blockchain node and the app server. Keep both open.
  2. 2Your browser opens http://localhost:8000. If it doesn't, type that address into Chrome or Edge.
  3. 3For presenting, press F11 for fullscreen.

First time on a new laptop? Install Python 3.12 and Node.js, then run setup.bat once before start.bat.

Basics

Getting around

Everything starts from the home page. You switch between the four kinds of users with the workspace button, the way you switch accounts in Slack.

1.1

The home page

  1. 1Workspace button. Shows who you're signed in as. Click it to switch.
  2. 2Get started scrolls to the workspace cards.
  3. 3The faint ▶ button opens the presenter tools (see section 2).
1.2

Switching workspace

Click the workspace button and pick an organisation. The navigation bar changes to match: lenders see Dashboard, Applications and Audit; a business sees My invoices and Get paid early; a buyer sees Confirmations.

1.3

Or pick a workspace from the home page

Section 2

Presenter tools

The presenter drawer fills the system with realistic activity. Each scenario submits real PDF documents (invoice, purchase order, delivery receipt) through the full pipeline, exactly as an upload would.

2.1

Open the drawer

  1. 1Press P on the keyboard, or click ▶ in the bottom-right corner. Click Submit to Shakti Bank on scenario 01.
  2. 2Reset data wipes everything and deploys a fresh contract. Do this before every presentation.
2.2

Pay out the genuine invoice

  1. 1The result appears on the card: auto-approved, score 86. Click Disburse & register on-chain. The bank pays out and the invoice is locked in the shared registry.
2.3

Try to finance it twice

  1. 1Click scenario 02. The same invoice, re-issued with a different number format, goes to Kaveri Capital. It's rejected: the on-chain registry shows Shakti Bank already financed it.

Then run 03 to 06. 03: inflated invoice with an edited PDF. 04: genuine but slow-paying buyer. 05: same delivery billed under a new number. 06: an invoice the buyer already paid.

Section 3

Lender guide

Switch to Shakti Bank. Every application arrives already verified, scored and priced.

3.1

Dashboard

  1. 1Portfolio numbers: paid out, live exposure, approval rate, ₹ of fraud blocked, average decision time.
  2. 2Needs your attention: applications waiting for a decision or a payout. Click one to open it.
  3. 3Blocked by Verbil: every rejected application with its reason and amount.
3.2

Reading an application

  1. 1Score ring: 0 to 100 with a grade. Green is approved, amber needs review, red is blocked.
  2. 2Decision in plain English, with the offer below it: advance %, rate, tenor, fees and net amount to the business.
  3. 3Evidence tabs: Credit pack, Cross-checks, Duplicate shield, Forensics, Risk score, Extracted data, Audit trail, Privacy proof, Verification record.
3.3

Credit pack

  1. 1The lender's one-page summary: what supports eligibility on the left, what still needs your review on the right, each with the evidence and a suggested action.
3.4

Cross-checks: why an invoice failed

  1. 1Each check names the rule applied, a confidence level and the discrepancy in rupees, down to the line. Here the invoice bills 150 units against an order of 100, and the pad price was raised from ₹400 to ₹450.
3.5

Duplicate shield

  1. 1Four layers: same file, same invoice fingerprint, look-alike invoices, and the shared on-chain registry. Layer 4 is the one that sees across lenders.
3.6

Payment reconciliation

  1. 1Compares what was charged (invoice), what was paid (bank statement) and what the seller claims is outstanding (statement of account). Here the buyer already paid, and the statement leaves the payment out.
3.7

Manual review and buyer confirmation

  1. 1Request buyer confirmation asks the buyer to acknowledge the invoice. It lands in their Confirmations inbox.
  2. 2Approve or Reject when you've reviewed it. Approved invoices get a Disburse button.
Section 4

Buyer guide

Switch to Orbit Retail (or Zenith Motors). Buyers see every invoice their suppliers are financing.

4.1

Confirm or dispute

  1. 1Confirm signs "we owe this" on the blockchain.
  2. 2The tag shows which lender asked. Dispute opens a short form (goods not received, wrong price, already paid, duplicate) and puts financing on hold.
4.2

What confirmation changes

  1. 1The supplier's risk score improves (here 53 → 65, grade C → B), which raises the advance it's offered.
  2. 2The invoice is marked Confirmed on-chain.
Section 5

Business guide

Switch to Acme Precision Parts.

5.1

Track your invoices

  1. 1Each invoice shows where it is: Submitted → Verified → Offer → Funded → Repaid, with a note on what happens next.
  2. 2Declined requests show the reason.
5.2

Get paid early

  1. 1Choose the lender and upload the tax invoice. Add the purchase order, delivery note, bank statement and statement of account for a stronger offer.
  2. 2Click Submit for financing. The decision appears in seconds.
Section 6

Proof & audit

Every decision can be proven to someone who doesn't trust Verbil.

6.1

Issue a verification record

As Shakti Bank, open the funded invoice INV-2026-1001, go to the Verification record tab and click Issue signed record. It lists the documents (as fingerprints), every rule applied and its result, and the decision. It's signed and anchored on-chain. Click Download record to save it.

  1. 1Verify this record now checks the signature and the on-chain anchor. Forge it changes the decision and shows the check failing.
6.2

Verify as an outsider

  1. 1Switch to Independent verifier and upload the record file (optionally with the documents you hold).
  2. 2The result: signature, anchor and whether the documents have changed since verification.
6.3

Share only what's needed

  1. 1In the Privacy proof tab, tick the fields to reveal (for example total, due date and buyer) and click Generate proof card.
  2. 2Verify as the recipient proves those fields belong to the original invoice without revealing anything else.
6.4

Audit trail

  1. 1As a lender, open Audit and click Verify everything.
  2. 2It recomputes every event, link, on-chain anchor and stored file.
6.5

Catching an insider

  1. 1In the presenter drawer, click Tamper a stored invoice and Rewrite a decision, then Verify integrity. It pinpoints the edited record, the on-chain mismatch and the altered file.

Demo script

About five minutes. Reset data first.

  1. Home page. One line on the problem, then "Choose your workspace".
  2. Press P → 01 → Disburse. "Verified, scored and priced in under a second."
  3. 02. "A second lender, clean books, and it's still blocked."
  4. 03, 05, 06, then the Shakti Bank dashboard: the blocked list fills with reasons and amounts.
  5. 04 → Request buyer confirmation → Orbit Retail → Confirm. Score 53 → 65.
  6. Acme → My invoices. The business's view of the same story.
  7. Verification record → Independent verifier. "Anyone can check this without trusting us."
  8. Tamper + Verify integrity. "Even we can't rewrite history."

Troubleshooting

The page doesn't load at localhost:8000
The app isn't running. Double-click start.bat and keep both terminal windows open.
The status chip says "Ledger offline"
The blockchain window was closed. Close both windows and run start.bat again. The app clears old data automatically when it detects a fresh chain.
Scenario 02 was approved instead of blocked
Invoice #1 hadn't been paid out yet. Current versions fund it automatically first; if you see this, click Reset data and run 01 → Disburse → 02.
Fonts look different
The typeface loads from Google Fonts. Offline, the app falls back to the system font and still works.
I want to start over
Press P, then Reset data.
