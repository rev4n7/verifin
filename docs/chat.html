Team explainer (problem, solution, Q&A)

What VeriFin is

An invoice-financing platform. It verifies an invoice in seconds against the purchase order, delivery receipt, bank statement and every other lender, prices the advance from the buyer's payment history, and records each step in a tamper-evident audit trail anchored on a blockchain.

Workspaces in the app

  • Lenders (Shakti Bank, Kaveri Capital): dashboard, applications, audit
  • Business (Acme): invoice tracker, upload
  • Buyers (Zenith, Orbit): confirm or dispute invoices
  • Independent verifier: check any record or document

Run it

  1. On the build laptop, double-click C:\Users\asus\verifin\start.bat.
  2. It opens the blockchain node and the app, then the browser at http://localhost:8000.
  3. Press P (or the faint ▶ bottom-right) for the presenter drawer with live scenarios and reset.

Stage flow

  1. Homepage → "Choose your workspace"
  2. P → run 01 → Disburse → run 02 (blocked on-chain)
  3. Run 03, 05, 06 → Shakti Bank dashboard shows blocked frauds
  4. Run 04 → request confirmation → switch to Orbit Retail → Confirm → score 53 → 65
  5. Acme's invoice timeline → signed record → public verifier
  6. Insider attacks → Verify integrity

Opening line for the judges

"Before funding an invoice, a lender needs four answers: is it real, was it delivered, will the buyer pay, and is it already financed elsewhere. Today those answers come from separate tools owned by separate parties, and nobody else can check them. VeriFin answers all four in seconds, explains each answer with evidence down to the line and the rupee, and produces a signed record that any lender, buyer or auditor can verify independently."

01Brief & plan

Team

Heyyy so we are in the middle of a hackathon and this is our problem statement:

Invoice Financing with Hybrid Audit Architecture

Small and medium-sized businesses face cash-flow constraints because customer payments can take weeks or months to arrive. Lenders offering invoice financing must confirm that an invoice is genuine, that goods or services were delivered, that the buyer is likely to pay, and that the invoice has not been financed elsewhere. Fragmented documents and manual checks lead to duplicate financing, manipulated invoices, delayed approvals, and disputes.

Your challenge: develop an intelligent invoice-financing platform that evaluates invoice authenticity, assesses financing risk, identifies potential duplication, and maintains a verifiable history of important events through a hybrid audit architecture. It could support: invoice data extraction from uploaded documents; cross-checks against purchase orders, delivery receipts, and contracts; duplicate invoice detection across repeated submissions; financing risk assessment from buyer payment history and receivables; hybrid audit (off-chain files with tamper-evident on-chain hashes); explainable verification and an automated financing workflow.

Goal: build a working prototype of a trustworthy invoice-financing platform that automates verification, detects duplicate or inconsistent claims, supports risk-based financing decisions, and keeps a tamper-evident audit history without exposing sensitive documents unnecessarily.

Claude

Proposed a pipeline: extract → 3-way match (invoice, PO, delivery) → duplicate check → risk score → decision → audit, with every step logged to a hash chain and anchored on-chain.

  • Canonical fingerprint: hash of seller GSTIN, buyer GSTIN, invoice number, amount and date, so a re-scanned copy still matches. Stored in a smart contract, so a second lender is blocked without seeing anyone's data.
  • Hybrid audit: encrypted files off-chain, hash-chained events, Merkle roots on-chain, and a live "tamper one byte" demo.
  • Explainable scorecard: every point traceable, turned into an advance rate and fee.

Asked for time left, stack constraints, and whether to start building.

Team

Team

Claude

Research findings that shaped the features:

  • Duplicate financing is a real, paid-for problem: vendors like MonetaGo sell closed fingerprint registries to banks and TReDS platforms. Ours is an open on-chain registry.
  • Edited PDFs leave traces (extra save sections, editor tools as producer, odd timestamps), so PDF forensics catches manipulated invoices.
  • Merkle selective disclosure lets a seller prove chosen fields without revealing the rest.

Published the team explainer: claude.ai/artifact/NciMW2nWF6AVx18u8gfR9C.

04"Doesn't this exist?"

Team

the idea already exists it seems

Claude

Parts of it do, which shows the problem is real. MonetaGo, TFM's Invoice Check and the TReDS platforms each cover one slice. VeriFin's edge: an open registry instead of a vendor one, a full pipeline instead of a dedup check alone, four duplicate layers, selective disclosure, on-chain buyer confirmation, and a provable audit trail.

Team

how does it differ from billacle?

Claude

Billacle is accounts-payable automation for a buyer's finance team: invoice capture, approvals, GST/ITC reconciliation, Tally sync. It decides whether a company should pay a bill. VeriFin decides whether a lender should advance money against one, across lenders that can't see each other. The only overlap is invoice extraction.

"AP tools like Billacle live on the buyer's side. In production, that's where our buyer confirmation would come from."

05Mentor feedback

Team
Feedback: where the idea could stand out

A. Evidence-backed trust score: show every check, its evidence, confidence and the reason for any failure.

B. Portable verification record: something another authorised party can check independently.

C. Verification that leads to a financing decision: a lender-ready evidence package.

Claude
  • A: every check now carries a rule ID and version, confidence, sources and the discrepancy in rupees, for example "Invoice is ₹77,500 (₹91,450 incl. GST) above what the PO and delivery support."
  • B: a signed (Ed25519), W3C-credential-style record anchored on-chain, plus tools/verify_record.py, which checks it offline against the contract. Forging any field fails.
  • C: a credit pack separating "what supports eligibility" from "what still needs lender review", with buyer acceptance, payment history, receivables and conditions before disbursal.

A real bug. Scenario 01 was approved but never disbursed, so nothing was on-chain yet, and the automatic setup step submitted a second copy instead of disbursing the first.
